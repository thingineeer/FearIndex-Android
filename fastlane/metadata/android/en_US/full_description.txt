Fear & Greed Index is an investment sentiment analysis app that lets you monitor both the Market Fear & Greed Index and the Crypto Fear & Greed Index at a glance. Track two distinct fear indices alongside major market indices to understand crowd psychology and make informed investment decisions.

[Two Fear & Greed Indices]
- Market Fear & Greed Index: Sentiment indicator for traditional financial markets
- Crypto Fear & Greed Index: Sentiment indicator for cryptocurrency markets
- Compare both indices side by side to grasp overall market dynamics

[Key Features]
- Real-time Market & Crypto Fear & Greed Index display
- Intuitive market sentiment visualization with gauge chart
- Historical data comparison (Previous close, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year ago)
- Trend analysis with history charts (1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y)
- Real-time major market indices (KOSPI, KOSDAQ, NASDAQ, S&P 500, Dow Jones)
- One-tap toggle between Market and Crypto indices

[Push Notifications]
- Instant alerts when Market fear index reaches your threshold
- Instant alerts when Crypto fear index reaches your threshold
- Customizable upper and lower threshold settings

[What is the Fear & Greed Index?]
A market sentiment indicator representing investor psychology on a scale from 0 (Extreme Fear) to 100 (Extreme Greed).
- 0-24: Extreme Fear
- 25-44: Fear
- 45-55: Neutral
- 56-75: Greed
- 76-100: Extreme Greed
